# Decode Labs UI/UX Design Internship

Design projects completed during my UI/UX Design Internship at Decode Labs (Oct to Nov 2026).

**Designer:** Kehinde Adeoluwa Paul
**Tools:** Figma, FigJam

## Project 1: The Empathy Map
**Description:** A user persona (Adaeze, 29, Marketing Manager, Lagos) and an empathy map for a fitness app aimed at busy office professionals.
**Key insight:** The real barrier isn't motivation, it's time and energy.
**How to view:** Design files are in Figma. Screenshots are posted on my LinkedIn.

## Project 2: The Blueprint (Wireframing)
**Description:** A low-fidelity e-commerce homepage wireframe in black, white and grey, focused on user flow, hierarchy and clear CTAs.
**Key decisions:** Z-pattern layout, one primary CTA in the hero, clean product grid, trust signals before the final CTA.
**How to view:** Design files are in Figma. Screenshots are posted on my LinkedIn.
